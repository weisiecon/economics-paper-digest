## 1. Weakly Supervised Adaptation of Scientific Embeddings for Exploratory Literature Mapping [AI for Science]

**Authors:** Roman Neruda, Petra Vidnerová, Jiří Šejnoha, Jakub Mihálik, Tomáš Marvan
**Date:** October 18, 2026
**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25413
**Summary:** The authors test whether lightly adapting a general-purpose scientific embedding model (SPECTER2) to a small, specialized corpus improves its usefulness for mapping research literature, using only citation-based metadata rather than hand-labeled similarity judgments. Applied to 704 papers on artificial and machine consciousness, the adapted embeddings spread out the similarity structure of the corpus and produce more modular, better-connected topic graphs than the unadapted model, lexical, or citation-based baselines. The work points to a cheap way to make AI literature-mapping tools more useful for niche research fields, though the authors are careful to note the evaluation is self-referential and limited to a single domain.
