## 1. When AI Classifies: What Counts as Public Administration? [AI for Science]

**Authors:** Shaoming Cheng, Laurie A. Schintler
**Date:** November 11, 2026 (v2; originally posted June 26, 2026)
**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05420
**Summary:** The authors compare how Web of Science and OpenAlex, using author-defined, citation-driven, and AI-assisted approaches, identify and bound public administration and AI-in-public-administration scholarship. The systems produce substantially different corpora rather than just larger or smaller versions of the same literature, differing in size, publication type, outlets, and thematic structure. The authors conclude that AI-assisted scholarly classification is interpretive and potentially self-reinforcing, shaping which research is visible and how a field's boundaries evolve, so human expert judgment remains necessary alongside it.
