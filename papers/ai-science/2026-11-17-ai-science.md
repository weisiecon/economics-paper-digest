## 1. Efficiency Gains in Social Science Research: A Prospective Silicon Sampling Protocol for Pre-Treatment and Code Optimization [AI for Science]

**Authors:** Noaïm Astitou, Etienne Aucouturier
**Date:** November 13, 2026
**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18436
**Summary:** The authors test using three LLMs (Sonnet 4.6, GPT 5.3, and Kimi 2.6) to generate synthetic "silicon sampling" survey responses ahead of real data collection, using a France Life Imaging network survey as a case study. They find the approach cuts code-development time substantially (85-90% code reuse) by letting researchers build and debug analysis pipelines on simulated data first, but its reliability depends on how predictable a survey's theme is, with synthetic data missing specialized or highly contextual response patterns. They frame this as extending prior "Phase 0" instrument-validation work into a general preprocessing step for scalable survey research.
