## 1. Clouds of Credit: Mapping the Financial Web Behind AI Compute [Firms & Competition]

**Authors:** Stijn Van Nieuwerburgh, Zijin Su
**Date:** first posted 2026-11-01
**Source:** https://www.nber.org/papers/w36032
**Summary:** The authors build a calibrated equilibrium model of the AI compute buildout and map the debt, equity, and non-recourse project-financing network that supports it, finding that large equity cushions currently protect creditors even though debt finances nearly 60% of the expansion and raises leverage. They show that equilibrium investment exceeds the socially optimal level because firms ignore how their own capacity decisions erode rivals' revenues, and that in a low-productivity scenario combined losses to shareholders and creditors could reach $16.8 trillion, with removing contractual payment spillovers among a handful of key firms cutting private losses by roughly 30%. The results suggest the AI compute race is simultaneously overbuilt relative to the social optimum and financially fragile, with losses concentrated and amplified through a small set of interlinked financing contracts.
