# AI Economics Papers — 2026-11-01

## 1. Who Owns Digital News, and Who Gets Cited: Measuring Media Concentration Across 77 Countries and Seven AI Systems [Firms & Competition]

**Authors:** Peter Carragher, Kathleen M. Carley
**Date:** first posted 2026-10-29
**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40164
**Summary:** The authors build an ownership dataset covering 9,761 digital news outlets to construct traffic-weighted media concentration indices for 77 countries, then compare these to the citation patterns of seven major AI systems (including Google's AI Overviews) when those systems answer news-related queries. They find that AI citation distributions are up to 2.08x more concentrated than underlying traffic, that outlet-level concentration measures understate true owner-level concentration by a median of 1.30x, and that if AI Overviews mediated all organic search, the resulting rise in US media concentration would dwarf that associated with heavily scrutinized media mergers. The paper also documents that AI systems disproportionately cite state-controlled outlets when prompted in a country's native language, raising concrete antitrust- and competition-relevant concerns about how AI-mediated discovery is reshaping market structure in digital news.
