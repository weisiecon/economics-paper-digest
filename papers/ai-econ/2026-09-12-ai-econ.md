# AI Economics Papers — 2026-09-12

## 1. AI Coding Tools and Digital Entrepreneurship: The Role of Software Expertise [Adoption & Diffusion]

**Authors:** Ruiqing Cao, Abhishek Bhatia
**Date:** first posted 2025-11-09; revised 2026-09-08
**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.06545
**Summary:** Using pre-LLM product descriptions to measure how exposed different digital-venture categories are to AI coding tools, then linking that exposure to venture launch, web-traffic and financing records, the authors trace how the post-2022 diffusion of AI coding assistants reshaped entry into digital entrepreneurship. Exposure to AI coding raised first-time venture launches after 2022Q4 but lowered new entrants' survival on average, and founders with prior software work experience broke from that pattern: they accounted for a disproportionate share of the new launches, largely avoided the survival penalty when product development was only partially rather than fully automated, and captured essentially all of the associated rise in financing. The result is a firm-level diffusion finding with a clear distributional edge: AI tools lower the technical barrier to starting a venture, but the durable value and financing gains from that lowered barrier still flow mainly to founders holding complementary software expertise.
