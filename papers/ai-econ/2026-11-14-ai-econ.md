# AI Economics Papers — 2026-11-14

## 1. Financing the AI Buildout [Productivity & Growth]

**Authors:** Stijn Van Nieuwerburgh
**Date:** first posted October 2026
**Source:** https://www.nber.org/papers/w35865
**Summary:** This paper sizes the capital needed for the US AI data-center buildout and traces how hyperscalers are structuring its financing, estimating that a 1 GW AI campus costs roughly $41 billion and that a 188 GW national buildout completed by 2032 would require nearly $9 trillion, or about 3.2% of GDP annually over 2025-2032. It documents that as capital expenditure needs outgrow hyperscalers' internal cash flow, financing is shifting from on-balance-sheet spending toward leases, joint ventures, project debt, private credit, securitization, and special-purpose vehicles. The paper argues these structures expand funding capacity but also raise asset-level leverage and obscure contingent obligations, creating investor exposure to tenant concentration, technological obsolescence, and uncertain residual values that matters for assessing systemic financial risk from the AI compute race.
