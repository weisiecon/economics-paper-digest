# AI Economics Papers — 2026-11-07

No qualifying papers today. The arXiv harvest (cat:econ.GN, econ.TH, cs.GT, cs.CY; 30-day window) returned 1,463 entries updated in-window out of 2,000 fetched, 458 passed the keyword filter, and 400 remained after removing IDs already covered in this digest's history. None of the surviving candidates met the relevance bar: the pool was dominated by AI-safety/governance, LLM-evaluation, and general mechanism-design/pricing-theory papers with no AI-market or AI-macroeconomic content, and the handful of Adoption & Diffusion-adjacent items (e.g. an AI-moderated market-research methodology paper, an AI-inference carbon-accounting method paper) were tools/methods papers rather than causal, structural, or welfare analyses of AI's economic effects.

## Excluded, borderline

- **AI-Moderated Interviews for Market Research and Digital Twins Calibration** (arXiv:2609.29143) — a well-designed pre-registered RCT (AI- vs human-moderated interviews), but its subject is a market-research methodology, not AI's effect on markets, firms, or aggregate outcomes.
- **Accounting for Purchased Artificial Intelligence Inference in Corporate Greenhouse Gas Inventories: A Token-Based Method** (arXiv:2606.10660) — a carbon-accounting method for AI inference purchases; touches the market for inference only as a measurement unit, with no market-structure or welfare analysis.
- **Data Governance for Generative and Agentic AI: Technology, Law, Economics, and Security** (arXiv:2610.33605) — a book-length survey covering an "economics of data governance" chapter among others, but descriptive/normative rather than a research-design-driven economics paper.
- **Can Artificial Intelligence Forecast Treatment Effects in Economics?** (arXiv:2610.38883) — evaluates LLMs as forecasters of economics-study treatment effects; about AI as a research tool for economics, not AI's effect on markets, firms, or welfare, so left to the ai-science digest.
