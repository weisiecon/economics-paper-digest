# AI & Labor Markets Papers — 2026-09-19

## 1. Linguistic Triggers of Gender and Racial Bias in Open-Weight LLMs Applied to Recruitment [Hiring]

**Authors:** Kosuke Kitahara, Nobuhiro Yamaguchi
**Date:** Submitted 2026-09-16
**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18106
**Summary:** The authors run the first systematic multi-model audit of six open-weight LLMs (Llama 3.2, Mistral, Gemma 3, Qwen 3, Phi 3, DeepSeek-R1) used in hiring pipelines, treating job-posting language as the experimental variable across controlled recruiter-simulation and job-seeker-simulation tasks. They find that agentic posting language depresses recruiter scores for female candidates while communal language partly reverses the penalty, and that coded-exclusion language suppresses non-White recruiter scores and deters non-White job-seeker personas from applying, with a label-ablation experiment isolating the demographic persona label as the primary causal driver. The results quantify a concrete, model-general discrimination channel in AI-mediated hiring and translate it into a pre-deployment audit protocol tied to EU AI Act and U.S. EEOC adverse-impact thresholds.
