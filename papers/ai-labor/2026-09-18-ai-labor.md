# AI & Labor Markets Papers — 2026-09-18

## 1. Linguistic Triggers of Gender and Racial Bias in Open-Weight LLMs Applied to Recruitment [Hiring]

**Authors:** Kosuke Kitahara, Nobuhiro Yamaguchi
**Date:** Submitted 2026-09-16
**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18106
**Summary:** Kitahara and Yamaguchi audit six open-weight LLMs (Llama 3.2, Mistral, Gemma 3, Qwen 3, Phi 3, DeepSeek-R1) across four controlled experiments that vary job-posting language and simulate both recruiter and job-seeker decisions. They find agentic posting language depresses recruiter scores for female candidates while communal language partly reverses the penalty, and that coded-exclusion language suppresses recruiter scores for non-White candidates at large effect sizes while also deterring non-White personas from applying at all; a label-ablation experiment isolates the explicit demographic persona as the causal driver. The results identify concrete linguistic levers of discriminatory hiring behavior in LLM-based recruitment tools and translate them into a pre-deployment audit protocol tied to EU AI Act and U.S. EEOC adverse-impact standards.
