## 1. Whose Voice Survives the Summary? A Voice-Retention Audit of LLM Employee Listening [Firm Adoption]

**Authors:** Thilo Tamme, Anton Hantel, Bijan Khosrawi-Rad
**Date:** Submitted 2026-09-30
**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38818
**Summary:** The authors build a Voice Retention/Representation Ratio metric and apply it to a real bilingual (English/German) corpus of 2,586 free-text employee survey responses and 45 actual leader-facing summaries from a global professional-services firm, auditing how its LLM summarization pipeline compresses employee feedback before it reaches management. They find employees withhold praise 82 times more often than criticism, that the pipeline filters by how often a concern is raised rather than by its sentiment (a concern voiced only once is dropped 86% of the time, and short or German-only content is lost on the same axis), and that controlling for frequency removes any independent effect of sentiment, meaning naive sentiment-only audits would miss the harm entirely. The paper exposes a concrete, previously unaudited mechanism by which routine firm-level adoption of LLM tools silently narrows which workers' voice actually reaches decision-makers, with direct implications for how organizations should audit AI-mediated internal communication.

Previously excluded from three earlier digests on the same grounds.
- **Prepared Or Unprepared? Evaluating Healthcare Workforce Readiness for Clinical Adoption of AI in Nigeria** (arXiv:2609.19096) — a cross-sectional attitudes/readiness survey of 761 clinicians; descriptive, no causal or market-outcome design. Previously excluded from three earlier digests on the same grounds.

Excluded for date uncertainty: both the NBER and IZA landing pages (opened this session) show only "September 2026" with no day, and the one specific day found in secondary news coverage (September 15) is a search-result snippet, which the task's anti-fabrication rule bars from being treated as authoritative; that date would in any case fall one day outside the 30-day window (cutoff 2026-09-16).
