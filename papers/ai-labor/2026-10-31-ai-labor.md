## 1. What is Work For? Labor, Politics, and Meaning in the Age of AI [Policy]

**Authors:** Julian Jacobs
**Date:** first posted 2026-10-29
**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39375
**Summary:** Using an original cross-national survey of 8,234 working-age adults in the UK, US, Denmark, and India, the author builds a "Work Values" Index to measure how much people are motivated by intrinsic, social, status, and purely financial rewards from their jobs. He finds these non-monetary motivations are large and evenly spread across income levels and occupational classes, and that a meaningful share of workers — especially the intrinsically motivated — would keep working or doing work-like activities even if given a guaranteed income well above their current salary. The results bear directly on AI labor-transition policy: a universal basic income sized to replace lost wages may still fail to replace the non-monetary benefits of work that AI displacement would take away.
